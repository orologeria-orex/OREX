Voivode Vlad Țepeș

Prince of Wallachia · Knight of the Order of the Dragon

The Order of the Dragon — the founding oath

Founded in 1408 by King Sigismund of Luxembourg, the Order of the Dragon was a chivalric fraternity dedicated to defending Christendom against Ottoman expansion. Vlad II Dracul, Vlad Țepeș’s father, was admitted into the Order, and the dragon emblem became both a family legacy and a political mission.

For Vlad III, the dragon is not ornament: it is an oath — discipline, loyalty, and the punishment of betrayal in an age of instability.

Quick facts

  • Born: 1431, Sighișoara (Transylvania)
  • Era: 15th century
  • Role: Ruler (Voivode) of Wallachia
  • Context: Ottoman pressure, internal rivalries
  • Symbol: The Order of the Dragon (1408)

Recommended tone: historical, sober, premium — no gothic/fantasy framing.

Transylvania — “Suisse des Carpates”

Medieval Transylvania, evoked today as “Suisse des Carpates”, was a land of fortresses, local autonomy, and rigorous organization. Within this Carpathian space Vlad Țepeș is shaped: a culture of order and endurance, at the frontier between worlds.

In the OREX® universe, the idea of Suisse des Carpates reinforces the identity of Comte des Carpates: rigor, precision, and European continuity.

Reign and the fortresses of power

Vlad Țepeș’s authority relied on strategic centers and fortifications: the Princely Court of Târgoviște, Poenari Fortress (a Carpathian bastion), and traditions associated with Snagov. For him, power was functional: order, control, stability.

Wars against the Ottomans

The confrontation with the Ottoman Empire involved asymmetric tactics, psychological warfare, and strict military discipline. The Night Attack at Târgoviște (1462) remains one of the boldest medieval actions in this region.

Betrayals, captivity, and fate

Internal rivalries, boyar betrayals, and great-power politics led to Vlad’s isolation. Captured and imprisoned for a period, he later returned to the arena, but his death (1476) closed a destiny that would, over time, be transformed into legend.

Beyond the myth

Vlad Țepeș has been reinterpreted through propaganda and fiction. Beyond “Dracula”, there remains the reality of a ruler who sought to impose order in a world of chaos.

For OREX®, Vlad Țepeș is a symbol of Carpathian rigor — and of time as a judge.
