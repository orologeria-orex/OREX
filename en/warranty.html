Orex® Warranty

Orex® watches are accompanied by a manufacturer’s warranty certificate confirming the authenticity of the product and the applicable service conditions in Romania and internationally.

Warranty duration & validity

Orex® provides a commercial warranty of twenty-four (24) months from the date of purchase, in accordance with the warranty certificate conditions and the applicable national consumer protection legislation.

The warranty is valid only if the Warranty Certificate and the International Warranty Card are correctly and completely filled in, dated, and stamped/sealed by an OREX authorized seller or distributor.

Territorial coverage

The warranty applies in Romania and internationally, through Orex® authorized partners (authorized sellers or distributors), based on a valid and properly completed warranty certificate/card.

What the warranty covers

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Proper functioning of the movement under normal conditions of use

What is not covered

  • Normal wear and tear and aesthetic deterioration (scratches, color aging, natural aging)
  • Damage caused by shocks, impacts, accidents or negligence
  • Unauthorized interventions, modifications or abusive/abnormal use
  • Water damage if water resistance (WR) conditions are not respected
  • Straps, bracelets, crystals and accessories

Product replacement

In the event of product replacement, the warranty for the replacement watch expires twenty-four (24) months from the original purchase date.

Service & recommendations

For repairs or maintenance, Orex® recommends contacting the authorized seller or distributor from whom the product was purchased and using authorized service centers only. Regular maintenance helps keep your Orex® watch in perfect working condition.
